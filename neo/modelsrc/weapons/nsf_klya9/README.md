# v_nsf_klya9 with segmented reloads - research branch

The Kyla revolver has no chamber to keep: a reload is one sequence for dry and partial. A reload interrupted after the
cylinder swung open (f24) resumes from the draw with the cylinder held open (`draw_resume_drop_dry`), or from the
hold by a reach (`reload_from_drop_dry`). Built with ntre's `proposals/0057-reload-cue-timing/tools/` and studiomdl.
